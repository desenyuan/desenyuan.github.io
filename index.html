I am a first-year Ph.D. student at the Graduate School of Information Sciences, Tohoku University, advised by Prof. Takayuki Okatani.

My research interests are 3D/4D vision and embodied AI — building AI that perceives, understands, and acts in the real world.

I am also interested in AI for space exploration and life extension. In my spare time, I enjoy reading science fiction.
